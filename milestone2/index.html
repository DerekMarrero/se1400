Website Summary:

What content will the site contain?

The site will contain match schedules, live league standings, corporate team profiles, player statistics, registration details, and photo galleries from local weekend tournaments.

Where will you get all of the content for your site (ie. images & text)?

Content will be gathered from local sports facility matches, team rosters provided by participating company HR departments, and original photographs taken during tournament matchdays.

Why should users care about the focus of the site?

Users, especially local employees and companies, will care because it promotes workplace wellness, team building, healthy competition, and great networking opportunities outside the office.

Why should a site be built for this?

A dedicated website is essential to streamline tournament administration, keep players updated with real-time fixtures, and give corporate teams a professional platform to showcase their stats.

What would you like users to do at your site?

We want users to check upcoming game fixtures, review league tables, register their company team for the upcoming season, and explore match highlights and photos.

What is the goal of the site?

The main goal is to serve as the ultimate digital hub for corporate amateur football, fostering a strong community spirit and encouraging an active lifestyle among local workers.
