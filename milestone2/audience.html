Target Audience Demographics

Geolocation:

The primary audience lives and works in Gran Canaria, specifically around Las Palmas and surrounding corporate business parks.

Age:

The target age range spans young professionals and adults between 20 and 45 years old.

Gender:

While historically male-dominated in corporate leagues, the platform welcomes mixed teams, with a primary current demographic profile of 80% male and 20% female participants and supporters.

Other Demographics:

Office workers, local company employees, sports enthusiasts, and corporate team managers looking to promote team-building and workplace health.
