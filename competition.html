Competitive Analysis

What our competitors are doing right:

Existing local sports platforms do a great job of keeping basic calendar fixtures updated and publishing final weekend match scores quickly.

What our competitors are doing wrong:

Many competitor websites have outdated, cluttered designs, lack mobile responsiveness, and do not include detailed player or corporate team statistics.

How we can do it better:

We will provide a cleaner, modern interface optimized for mobile devices, offer real-time standings, and highlight individual corporate player achievements to build a tighter community.
